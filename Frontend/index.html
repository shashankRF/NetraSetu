SYSTEM INTELLIGENCE

From Disaster Scenario to Evidence-Linked Analysis

NetraSetu connects scenario inputs with domain-aware document retrieval, source-linked evidence, structured analysis, and risk-aware governance. Each stage has a distinct role, helping users inspect the information behind a finding and understand where human review is needed.

How NetraSetu Works

The architecture separates document retrieval, evidence presentation, analytical processing and governance controls. This separation makes the workflow easier to inspect, test and improve.

Core Intelligence Components

Knowledge Base & Document Ingestion

Relevant disaster-management plans, policies, guidelines and other approved source documents are prepared and organised for retrieval.

Domain-Aware Retrieval

The existing BGE-M3-based retrieval pipeline searches the available knowledge base for passages relevant to the user's query or selected scenario.

Evidence & Analytical Review

Retrieved passages are presented with available document and clause references. The analytical workflow uses this evidence to support scenario-specific findings and highlight areas that need verification.

Risk & Governance Engine

The existing deterministic governance engine evaluates configured rules and evidence-related conditions to produce risk classifications, escalation information or review requirements, according to its actual implementation.

Every Finding Should Be Inspectable

NetraSetu is designed to make supporting evidence easier to inspect. Users should be able to follow available citations to the relevant source document and verify the underlying passage.

Where supported by the existing application, users can review the analysis, examine evidence quality, and make an authorised decision. A system-generated finding must not be presented as automatically verified merely because it contains a citation.

Designed to Be Tested, Not Just Demonstrated

NetraSetu can be evaluated through controlled disaster scenarios, retrieval relevance, citation correctness, processing performance, governance-rule behaviour and documented failure cases.

The reliability of its findings depends on source quality, retrieval performance, scenario assumptions and appropriate human verification. Real-time data integration and expanded operational deployment remain separate development steps unless verified in the current implementation.

PROJECT INFORMATION

About NetraSetu

NetraSetu is an evidence-driven disaster-management intelligence prototype designed to help users retrieve relevant requirements from disaster-management documents, examine supporting evidence and support structured, risk-aware decision-making.

The project focuses on traceable evidence, scenario-based analysis and improved information accessibility for disaster-management stakeholders.

Built by Shashank Kotnala

NetraSetu is being developed by Shashank Kotnala, an Electronics and Communication Engineering student interested in telecommunications, disaster resilience, evidence intelligence and responsible technology for public benefit.

Project purpose

NetraSetu is being prepared for the Viksit Bharat Young Leaders' Dialogue (VBYLD) 2027 — Hack for Social Cause. The project explores how document intelligence, evidence traceability and structured governance support could help disaster-management stakeholders make better-informed decisions. This describes the project's intended purpose and competition context; it does not imply official endorsement, selection or partnership.

Current status

The current prototype includes document retrieval, source-linked evidence, scenario-based analysis and dashboard review workflows where implemented. Production authentication, live satellite analysis, operational government deployment, real-time inter-agency communications and quantified social impact are not claimed.

Multi-Jurisdiction Intelligence

One intelligence engine. Portable, sovereign jurisdiction profiles.

A global technical standard defines the engineering protocol. But legal obligations, mandatory reporting windows, and security conformity depend entirely on national jurisdiction. NetraSetu applies the same rigorous reasoning across sovereign regulatory profiles without ever silently substituting one jurisdiction for another.

      Experimental AI Research

      A Research Platform for Evidence-Grounded AI & Governance.

      NetraSetu is an experimental research platform investigating how artificial intelligence can reason over high-stakes, safety-critical, and legally binding systems. Rather than building another generative chatbot, our research focuses on verifiable retrieval, consequence-driven governance, and traceable human-AI decision systems.

      System Theory

      The 7-Layer Conceptual Stack

      Every query moves through seven explicit architectural boundaries. Each layer answers a distinct question before passing structured state to the next:

      01 · KnowledgeCorpus Layer
      "What does the system know?"

      Multi-domain indexed knowledge bases (Telecom, Maritime, Defence) with chunk-level metadata and verified document provenance.

      02 · RetrievalVector Layer
      "Which evidence is relevant?"

      BGE-M3 1024-dim dense embeddings and FAISS index search with quota-based multi-KB candidate pooling.

      03 · EvidenceValidation Layer
      "Is the evidence sufficient, applicable, and trustworthy?"

      Provenance checking, absence safeguards (preventing false negative inferences), and dual counter-evidence retrieval.

      04 · ReasoningMulti-Agent Layer
      "What does the evidence actually imply?"

      Specialized analytical roles (Network, Compliance, Policy, Gap) decomposing evidence without unilateral authority.

      05 · RiskEvaluation Layer
      "How consequential is the decision?"

      Deterministic Risk Engine scoring decision severity, evidence certainty, operational impact, and required authority tiers.

      06 · GovernancePolicy Invariants
      "Who should be allowed to make the decision?"

      Inviolable governance rules enforcing mandatory human sign-off on critical risks, regulatory notices, and operational commands.

      07 · Human DecisionAuthority Layer
      "When should AI stop and a human take over?"

      A qualified reviewer inspects verified evidence, confirms, modifies, or rejects the assessment, recorded to the audit trail.

      Current Implementation

      Active Research & Technical Capabilities

      Operational components currently running in the NetraSetu engine:

      BGE-M3 Dense Embeddings

      1024-dimensional semantic vectors mapping technical specifications and legal statutes into a unified representation space.

      FAISS Sub-Millisecond Retrieval

      High-performance vector index indexing 14,143 main knowledge chunks, supporting instant nearest-neighbor candidate pooling.

      Dedicated Sovereign Knowledge Bases

      Dedicated indices for Maritime Comms (273 chunks) and Defence Technical Analysis (397 chunks) preventing domain cross-contamination.

      Jurisdiction-Aware Multi-KB Routing

      Dynamic evidence filtering ensuring national inquiries retrieve only legally applicable statutory frameworks alongside global standards.

      Absence-of-Evidence Safeguards

      Deterministic validation ensuring the system never converts unretrieved text into an affirmative claim that a regulation does not exist.

      Dual Counter-Evidence Engine

      Dedicated retrieval and classification surfacing limiting findings (e.g., valid signatures, clean SBOMs) that prevent premature attribution.

      Deterministic Risk Engine

      Mathematical risk scoring based on evidence sufficiency, impact severity, and governance invariants, independent of generative LLM drift.

      Append-Only Audit Recording

      SHA-256 verifiable JSONL records logging query, candidate evidence, agent findings, risk assessments, and human review decisions.

      GPT-OSS 120B Constrained Synthesis

      Grounded open-weights synthesis strictly conditioned on cited evidence IDs, preventing speculative hallucination.
